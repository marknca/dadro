The Modular 4-channel DRO

=

+

DRO:int4 is an interface kit that makes a flexible DRO (Digital Read-Out) out of an old PC and cheap Chinese digital scales or.
The DRO system can be used for a mill, a lathe or any tool machine that needs accurate positioning.
The interface connects through a serial connection (RS 232) to a PC or laptop that is used as a display.
