I hate SPAM, you hate SPAM. So why would you subscribe to yet another newsletter?
Because I promise you that I won't flood you with merchandbuzzing-"information".

What you will get are just notes about changes of versions or new versions, additions to functionality, fixes. No long text to wade through, just short excerpts with links to the verbose information here.

How do you subscribe?
Send an email to subscribe [at] yadro [dot] de (sorry SPAM-bots) with the subject "subscribe". You'll get a response-eMail you have to confirm (double opt-in). If you no longer want to subscribe, send an eMail with "unsubscribe" in the subject.
I won't give away your eMail-address to anybody, not even to YADRO-dealers. Promised!
